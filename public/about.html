About the project

Everything in its place

Mise is a student project for the Web Application Programming course at the Romanian-American University.

Mise en place is a French kitchen phrase that means “everything in its place” — every ingredient prepared and ready before cooking starts.

That's the idea behind our app: one tidy place for all your recipes. Add your own, find any dish by name or ingredient in seconds, and browse by category when you need inspiration.

What you can do

Save recipes

Add, edit and delete recipes with ingredients, steps and a photo.

Search instantly

Find a dish by its name or by what's already in your fridge.

Filter by category

Breakfast, soups, mains, desserts — browse exactly what you're in the mood for.
