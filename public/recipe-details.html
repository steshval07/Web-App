Creamy tomato soup

Sweet roasted tomatoes, garlic and a splash of cream — a velvety bowl of comfort that tastes even better the next day.

Edit Delete

Time

35 min

Servings

4

Level

Easy

Category

Soups

Ingredients 8 items

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Halve the tomatoes, quarter the onion and place them on a tray with the garlic. Drizzle with olive oil, season and roast for 25 minutes.

  2. Tip everything into a pot, pour in the stock and bring to a gentle simmer for 5 minutes.

  3. Add most of the basil and blend with a stick blender until silky. Stir in the cream and adjust the seasoning.

  4. Ladle into bowls, top with the remaining basil and a swirl of olive oil. Great with toasted bread.
